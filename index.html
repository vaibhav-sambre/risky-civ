RISKYCIV

Conquer territories. Build your empire. Outsmart the AI.

🗺️

Territory Control

You and the AI each start with 15 of 30 territories on a procedurally generated map. Expand by conquering enemy lands.

🔄

Turn Structure

Each turn has two phases: Action Phase (spend 3 actions) then Attack Phase (unlimited battles). After you, the AI takes its turn.

🃏

Card System

You draw 1 card per turn (up to 10). Cards grant powerful bonuses or impose penalties. Playing a card costs 1 action + 💰 money.

🔬

Tech Tree

Spend 🔬 Research to unlock technologies across Military, Economic, and Science branches. Each unlock costs 1 action.

1

🪖 Deploy Troops

Choose how many troops to deploy to any owned territory. Costs 3 ⚙️ Production per troop. Uses 1 action.

2

🏗️ Build Structure

Build a Factory 🏭, University 🎓, or Bank 🏦 on an owned territory. Each costs ⚙️ + 💰. Uses 1 action.

3

🃏 Play a Card

Play a card from your hand. All cards are positive — they cost 💰 money (shown on the card). Global event cards trigger automatically when drawn and affect all players! Uses 1 action.

4

🔬 Research Tech

Unlock a technology from the tech tree using 🔬 Research points. Technologies provide permanent upgrades. Uses 1 action.

⚙️

Production

+1 per territory, +2 per Factory. Spent on deploying troops (3 per troop) and building structures.

🔬

Research

+1 per territory, +2 per University. Spent on unlocking technologies in the tech tree.

💰

Money

+1 per territory, +2 per Bank. Spent on playing cards and building structures. Also pays troop upkeep (0.5 per troop per turn).

🌍

Continent Bonuses

Control all territories in a continent for bonus resources each turn. Larger continents give more rewards.

🎲

Dice Combat

Attack with up to 3 dice, defender rolls up to 2 dice. Highest dice are compared — ties go to the defender.

⚔️

Attack Phase

After spending your actions, enter the attack phase. Attack any adjacent enemy territory from your own. No limit on number of battles!

🛡️

Garrison Rule

Every territory must keep at least 1 troop. You can only attack with troops above the minimum.

⚓

Sea Routes & Continents

The map is divided into island continents separated by sea. To attack across continents via sea routes, you must research 🚢 Naval Supremacy in the Military tech tree.

🗺️

Territory Domination

Control 75% of all territories (38 out of 50) to achieve military victory.

🔬

World Domination Tech

Research the ultimate World Domination technology at the end of the Science branch. Requires 50 🔬.

💡 Pro Tip: Balance expansion with economy! Overextending without Banks for upkeep will cause your troops to desert.
